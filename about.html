About Me

I’m Nhlanhla — a self-taught web designer and front-end developer who believes in clean design, sharp code, and quiet execution. This page is a quick peek into who I am when I’m not pushing pixels or crafting user interfaces.

From Curiosity to Craft

My journey into tech started with curiosity — a curiosity about how websites worked, what made them feel smooth, and how they came to life. With no formal training, I dove headfirst into HTML, CSS, and JavaScript tutorials, constantly experimenting and building things from scratch. Over time, what began as raw interest turned into a passion for creating beautiful, functional websites.

As I sharpened my skills, I picked up tools like Tailwind CSS, React, and WordPress — turning ideas into responsive, conversion-focused experiences. I don’t just design to impress, I design to solve problems, tell stories, and make the web feel more human. Every site I build reflects my eye for detail and my love for minimal, purpose-driven design.
